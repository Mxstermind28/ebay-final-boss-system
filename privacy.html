Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026

eBay Final Boss System is a privately operated application designed to assist with eBay product research, inventory management, listing management, and related seller operations.

Information We Access

When authorized through eBay, the application may access information necessary to provide its functionality, including listing, inventory, account, order, and seller-related information permitted by the user and eBay APIs.

How Information Is Used

Information accessed through eBay is used only to provide the application's intended functionality, such as product research, inventory management, listing creation, listing monitoring, and other authorized seller operations.

Data Sharing

Personal information obtained through authorization is not sold or rented to third parties.

Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect credentials and information handled by the application. Authentication credentials, client secrets, passwords, and OAuth tokens are not intentionally published in the application's public source code.

User Authorization

Access to eBay account information requires authorization through eBay's authentication system. Users may revoke authorization in accordance with eBay's available account and application controls.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as the application develops or its data-handling practices change.

Contact

Questions regarding this Privacy Policy may be directed to the application operator through the application's official repository.
